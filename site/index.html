Why I went back to a paper notebook

October 28, 2026

Every notes app I have tried eventually asks me to reorganize my thoughts to suit its database. Paper never does. This month I moved my daily planning back to a pocket notebook, and the interesting part is not nostalgia — it is the friction. Writing by hand forces a first draft of every decision.

The apps are still better for search and sharing. But for thinking, slow wins more often than I expected.

A small defense of boring tools

October 12, 2026

The tools that survive in my workflow share three traits: they start fast, they fail loudly, and their files outlive the company that made them. Anything else is a rental, and rentals should be priced accordingly.
